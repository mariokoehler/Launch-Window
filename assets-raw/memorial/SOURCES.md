# Memorial photos: sources

Real historical photos used on the Museum's memorial plaques (converted to
`assets/ui/portraits/` by `assets-raw/portraits/make_portrait_assets.py`).

## laika.jpg

- **What:** Laika, the dog flown on Sputnik 2 (November 1957), in her flight harness.
- **Source:** a 1957 Soviet press photo, credited to **TASS** (the Soviet news agency) on the
  website the user found it on (2026-10-05). Cropped to a square by the user.
- **Licence: not established.** Commercial agencies (AFP, Heritage Images) still sell licences for
  this photo, so it shouldn't be assumed to be public domain. Fine while the repo is private.
  **Before the repo goes public:** find a copy with a clear free licence (Wikimedia Commons lists
  each file's licence), or replace it with original art.
