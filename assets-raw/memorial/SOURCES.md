# Memorial photos: sources

Real historical photos used on the Museum's memorial plaques (converted to
`assets/ui/portraits/` by `assets-raw/portraits/make_portrait_assets.py`). Every photo here needs a
licence that allows a public repo; the credits players see are in `assets/CREDITS.md`.

## laika.jpg

- **What:** Laika, the dog flown on Sputnik 2 (November 1957).
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons (2026-10-05), cropped to a square by the user. It replaces an
  earlier TASS press photo whose licence wasn't established.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Allows any use, including in a public repo,
  provided the author is credited, the licence is linked, and changes are noted.
- **Changes made:** cropped square, converted to greyscale, resized to 192x192.
- **Still to fill in:** the Commons file page URL and the author exactly as that page names them.
  Copy both here and into `assets/CREDITS.md`.
