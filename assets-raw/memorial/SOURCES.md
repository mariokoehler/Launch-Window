# Memorial photos: sources

Real historical photos used on the Museum's memorial plaques (converted to
`assets/ui/portraits/` by `assets-raw/portraits/make_portrait_assets.py`). Every photo here needs a
licence that allows a public repo; the credits players see are in `assets/CREDITS.md`.

## laika.jpg

- **What:** Laika, the dog flown on Sputnik 2 (November 1957).
- **Source:** Wikimedia Commons (2026-10-05), cropped to a square by the user. It replaces an
  earlier TASS press photo whose licence wasn't established.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0,
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Allows any use, including in a public repo,
  provided the author is credited, the licence is linked, and changes are noted.
- **Changes made:** cropped square, converted to greyscale, resized to 192x192.
- **File page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Laika_in_1957.jpg ("Laika in 1957").
- **Author, as the page names it:** "Музей космонавтики / Главархив Москвы" (Museum of
  Cosmonautics / Main Archive of Moscow), originally published on Mos.ru,
  https://www.mos.ru/news/item/94754073/. The page asks for attribution to Mos.ru.
- **Description on the page:** "The dog Laika before her orbital flight. From the collections of
  the Museum of Cosmonautics."
- **Credit given:** in `assets/CREDITS.md`.

## Animals that came home (2026-10-06)

Found by the user on Wikipedia, each with a `.license` file holding the file page's link. Cropped
square to 256x256 by the user; the converter makes them greyscale at 192x192. Credits for all of
them are in `assets/CREDITS.md`.

### belka_strelka.jpg

- **What:** the dogs Belka and Strelka after their orbital flight on Korabl-Sputnik 2,
  22 August 1960.
- **File page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Belka_and_Strelka.jpg
- **Author:** Museum of Cosmonautics / Main Archive of Moscow, photographers Sergei Preobrazhensky
  and Nikolai Sitnikov; originally published on Mos.ru, https://www.mos.ru/news/item/94754073/.
- **Licence:** CC BY 4.0. Credit Mos.ru, link the licence, note the changes.

### chernushka.jpg

- **What:** Chernushka, flown on Korabl-Sputnik 4 (9 March 1961), as displayed in the Pauls
  Stradins Museum of the History of Medicine, Riga (photographed 3 August 2023).
- **File page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vostok_Chernushka_144534898HDR.jpg
- **Author:** Michał Derela (Wikimedia user Pibwl), own work.
- **Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (also 3.0, 2.5, 2.0, 1.0). Credit the author, link the licence, and
  share the changed version under the same licence.

### ham.jpg

- **What:** the chimpanzee Ham being fitted into the Mercury-Redstone 2 capsule, January 1961.
- **File page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ham_the_chimp_(cropped).jpg
- **Author:** NASA, photo S63-20801.
- **Licence:** public domain (made by NASA).

### enos.jpg

- **What:** the chimpanzee Enos with his handler before the Mercury-Atlas 5 flight, 1961.
- **File page:** https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mercury-Atlas_5_Enos_with_handler.jpg
- **Author:** NASA, photo S61-04540.
- **Licence:** public domain (made by NASA).

### zvezdochka.jpg: not usable

- **File page:** https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Файл:Zvyozdochka.jpg
- **Why not:** a non-free file, a still from the documentary "Отряд космических дворняг" with
  the author unknown, kept on Russian Wikipedia under fair use for its article only. It mustn't go
  in the repo or the game; Zvezdochka keeps an empty photo square until a free photo turns up.
